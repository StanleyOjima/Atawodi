    03Doctrine

    The laws this work obeys.

    Operating principles, each tested in a real system and held with a stated confidence. When one changes, the revision shows.

      Law IRevised Mar 2026

      Observability is a contract.

      Name whose truth is being observed, how it is emitted, and which decision it supports.

      Tested in
      Its limit
      Telemetry nobody acts on is a cost, not a contract.
      Confidence
      High

      Law IISince 2024

      Incentives are architecture.

      A system behaves the way it rewards, even when the interface says otherwise.

      Tested in
      Its limit
      Incentives explain drift. They do not excuse a confusing interface.
      Confidence
      High

      Law IIIRevised Oct 2026

      Evidence should outlive the run.

      A run that succeeds without leaving durable proof creates fragile confidence.

      Tested in
      Its limit
      Keep what someone will check, not everything that happened.
      Confidence
      Very high

      Law IVRevised Sep 2026

      AI should compress cognition, not replace judgment.

      Agents may execute and gather proof. Ownership and decisions stay explicit.

      Tested in
      Its limit
      Some judgment is cheap enough to delegate. Say which, in advance.
      Confidence
      Evolving

      Law VSince 2025

      Trust must be legible.

      Claims, identity, reputation and proof need visible mechanisms and visible limits.

      Tested in
      Its limit
      Legible is not exposed. Show the mechanism, not the data.
      Confidence
      High
